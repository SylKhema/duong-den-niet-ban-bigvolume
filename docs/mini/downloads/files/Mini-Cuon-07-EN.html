Conditional Relations According to the Paṭṭhāna.

Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa.

Evamettha khandhāyatanadhātupaccayapaccavekkhaṇavasenapi asammohasampajaññaṃ veditabbaṃ. (Majjhima Commentary I, p. 268.)

Here, clear comprehension without delusion should also be understood by reviewing the aggregates, bases, elements and conditions.

This section on clear comprehension without delusion presents four methods: (1) by the five aggregates, (2) by the twelve bases, (3) by the eighteen elements, and (4) by examining conditional relations according to the Paṭṭhāna.

The great commentators instructed practitioners to exert themselves by these methods to attain knowledge of clear comprehension without delusion. In accordance with their guidance, this section on conditional relations according to the Paṭṭhāna explains how to examine and discern causal phenomena by the Paṭṭhāna method. It is intended for good people who wish to broaden their understanding through the way the Paṭṭhāna presents the teaching.
